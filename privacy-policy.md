# Privacy Policy for Remindly

Last updated: April 2026

Remindly we operates the Remindly mobile application.

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our app.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information.

All reminder data (such as titles, dates, and categories) is stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage systems.

We do not store your data on external servers.

## 2. Purchases

Remindly offers in-app purchases (monthly and yearly subscriptions) using Apple's StoreKit.

We do not collect or store your payment information. All transactions are handled securely by Apple.

## 3. Notifications

Remindly uses local notifications to remind you about tasks and events.

These notifications are scheduled and managed entirely on your device.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties.

## 5. Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple's frameworks such as:
- StoreKit (for purchases)
- UserNotifications (for reminders)

These services operate under Apple's privacy policies.

## 6. Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by Apple's security mechanisms.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Remindly is not intended for children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page.
